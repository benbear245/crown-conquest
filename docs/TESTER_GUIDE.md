# Crown Conquest — Tester Guide (test build v0.2.1)

Thanks for testing! Crown Conquest is a real-time territory game: grow your troops, grab land, and capture enemy Crowns while protecting your own. A match takes about 7–12 minutes.

## Install

**Android phone (Android 7 or newer, 64-bit)**
1. Open the file `CrownConquest-v0.2.1.apk` on your phone (from the email, chat or download link you got).
2. Android will ask to allow installing apps from this source (your browser, Files app, or chat app). Allow it.
3. If Play Protect warns that the app is unknown, tap **Install anyway** (it's a test build that isn't on the Play Store yet).
4. Open **Crown Conquest**. It plays sideways (landscape).

**Windows PC**
1. Extract `CrownConquest-v0.2.1-windows.7z` (Windows 11: right-click → Extract All; Windows 10: use the free 7-Zip app), then double-click `CrownConquest.exe`.
2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway** (the test build isn't signed for Windows).
3. Use the mouse: click = tap, hold the button = long-press, drag = move the map, scroll wheel = zoom.

## What to try (about 45 minutes)

1. **Tutorial:** tap **Start tutorial** on the welcome banner (or **Tutorial** at the bottom) and follow the steps (about 5 minutes). Did each step make sense? Did the arrow point at the right thing?
2. **First real match:** tap **Quick play**. Try to win, or at least survive past 5:00. Afterwards, read the recap: the land graph, best moment, XP and level bar.
3. **Match setup:** tap **Change map and bots**, change the map size, type, number of bots and difficulty. Back on the home screen, the line under Quick play should now show your choice. Was it clear where everything is on the home screen?
4. **Daily Challenge:** play it once and check your score appears on the menu. Tell us your score!
5. **Customize and Progress:** pick a colour, pattern, Crown icon, title and victory effect; check they show in your next match. Look at the trophy shelf and today's challenges.
6. **Building:** long-press your own land and build a Fort, a Barracks and a Watchtower. Try Wall mode.
7. **Spying:** long-press a rival's land. Scout them, then Spy (unlocks at 2:00). Offer a truce.
8. **Your Crown:** tap **♛ Crown**: buy a Keep upgrade, try Disinformation, and after 3:00 move your Crown.
9. **Shrines:** find a glowing diamond (◆) on the map and capture it.
10. **Settings:** open **Settings** on the home screen (or **☰ Menu** during a match) and try the colour-blind palette and the left-handed layout.
11. **Online (needs 2 devices on the same Wi-Fi):** one taps **Host a game** and reads out the big address shown; the other taps **Join a game**, types it in and taps **Join**; the host taps **Start match**. Keep the host's app open during the match.

## What to tell us

Please send a short message after each session:

- Your device (phone model, or Windows PC).
- Did it install and start without problems?
- Was anything confusing in the first 2 minutes?
- Did anything feel unfair, too hard or too easy? Were any bots unbeatable or useless?
- Was there a stretch where nothing interesting happened? About when (match time)?
- Did text, buttons or panels ever overlap, get cut off or feel too small?
- Did levelling up and unlocking things make you want to play another match?
- Anything you loved?

**If something goes wrong:** open **☰ Menu → Report a problem**. It copies your device details and the game's recent log; paste it into your message and say what you were doing just before. If the game froze or closed, reopen it and do the same straight away.

## Known limits of this build

- Online play works only between devices on the same Wi-Fi network (not over the internet yet), and the host must keep the app open.
- Sounds and music are simple placeholders.
- Your progress (level, unlocks, trophies) is saved only on this device. Uninstalling the app erases it; installing a newer test build over the old one keeps it.
- Online play needs everyone on v0.2.0 or newer; older builds can't join.
- Balance is still being tuned: some matches may reach the 15:00 time limit.
- There's no saved progress, levels or unlocks yet.
